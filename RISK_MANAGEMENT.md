# Risk Management

## FundingPips Challenge Limits

| Account | Daily Loss | Overall DD | Target |
|---------|------------|------------|--------|
| 10K | $500 (5%) | $1000 (10%) | $800 |
| 50K | $2500 (5%) | $5000 (10%) | $4000 |
| 100K | $5000 (5%) | $10000 (10%) | $8000 |

## Position Sizing
Risk per trade = 1% of balance
Volume = Risk / (SL points × Value per point)


## Risk Rules

1. Never risk more than 1% per trade
2. Hard stop at 5% daily loss
3. Hard stop at 10% overall drawdown
4. Only one position at a time
5. SL always mandatory
